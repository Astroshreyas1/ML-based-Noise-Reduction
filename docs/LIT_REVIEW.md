# Literature review — dataset generation & acquisition for defence speech enhancement / AI-ANC

Date: 2026-09-11. Scope: only the *data* side of the problem statement ("a scalable dataset pipeline for generating realistic noisy-clean speech pairs"). Model architecture and deployment are out of scope. Hackathon framing: every technique is judged on (a) does it change what the model learns, (b) can it be done in hours, (c) does it give us a claim a judge can verify.

## 1. How the field builds enhancement training data

**Dynamic (on-the-fly) mixing is the standard.** The DNS challenges (Microsoft, 2020-2023) ship a synthesizer that mixes clean speech, noise and RIRs at an SNR drawn from roughly [-5, 20] dB and convolves a fraction (~20 %) of utterances with RIRs; the URGENT challenges (2024, Interspeech 2025) go further and simulate *during* training with official scripts, covering seven distortion types: additive noise, reverberation, clipping, band-limiting, codec artefacts, packet loss and wind noise. Reported benefit: the model never sees the same mixture twice and the recipe can change without regenerating anything. [DNS 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.08662), [DNS 2023](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/deep-noise-suppression-challenge-icassp-2023/), [URGENT 2024](https://urgent-challenge.github.io/urgent2024/), [URGENT 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23212), [APSIPA dynamic mixing](https://www.apsipa.org/proceedings/2022/APSIPA%202022/WedPM2-2/1570831206.pdf).

**Lessons from URGENT 2024** (post-hoc analysis): distortion diversity matters more than raw hours; clipping and codec/packet-loss are the distortions most often missing from academic pipelines and most common in deployment; the winning systems were discriminative (mask/mapping) models trained on dynamically simulated data. [Lessons learned](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.01611).

**Benchmarks.** VoiceBank+DEMAND is the classic single-channel benchmark but is easy (min test SNR 2.5 dB, few noise types); WHAMR! adds real ambient noise plus reverberation and is used for out-of-domain checks. Our eval design should mirror that split: an in-domain frozen set and a harder out-of-domain one. [VB-DEMAND](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/voicebank-demand-dataset), [WHAM/WHAMR](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/335830057_WHAM_Extending_Speech_Separation_to_Noisy_Environments).

**Soundscape sequencers.** Scaper (2017) is the reference tool for injecting foreground *events* into a background at a chosen SNR with start/end labels, using LUFS loudness. Its two ideas transfer directly: events are placed and labelled, and event level is set relative to the background rather than by a global SNR. [Scaper](https://www.justinsalamon.com/uploads/4/3/9/4/4394963/salamon_scaper_waspaa_2017.pdf).

## 2. Sources for the noise piles

**Military Audio Dataset (MAD).** 8,075 clips, ~12 h, seven classes (communication, gunshot, footsteps, shelling, vehicle, helicopter, fighter), CC BY 4.0, extracted from YouTube military training videos, explicitly "enriched with authentic background noises such as wind, behavioural sounds and shouting". Consequences: (i) `communication` is speech and must never be a noise source; (ii) every other class needs a voice screen because of the shouting; (iii) all transients are lossy-compressed. Use for stationary/non-stationary classes and for *evaluation* realism of impulsive classes. [Nature Sci. Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-03511-w), [GitHub](https://github.com/kaen2891/military_audio_dataset), [Kaggle mirror](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/junewookim/mad-dataset-military-audio-dataset).

**Gunshots, real.** (a) Multi-firearm multi-orientation range dataset: 2,148 files, four firearms, several edge devices around an outdoor range, CSV with per-shot timestamps — the best open source of uncompressed real transients with labels. [Zenodo 7004819](https://zenodo.org/records/7004819), [Data in Brief](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10114508/). (b) UrbanSound8K `gun_shot` (~374 clips, Freesound field recordings, CC BY-NC) — ESC-50 has **no** gunshot class, a correction to our earlier plan. [UrbanSound8K](https://urbansounddataset.weebly.com/). (c) BGG / "Enemy Spotted" PUBG in-game gun dataset, 2,195 samples, 37 guns, five directions — mastered game audio; useful for classification, not for learning waveform signatures. [arXiv 2210.05917](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05917). (d) C3GD caliber-classification gunshot dataset (2026). [arXiv 2606.18135](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.18135).

**Gunshots, physics.** Maher's acoustic gunshot modelling establishes the two-component signature: muzzle blast (Friedlander overpressure wave) and the supersonic ballistic shockwave (N-wave) that arrives first for rifle rounds; both are broadband and both are routinely clipped by microphones near the source. Procedural synthesis based on physically motivated models is an accepted way to produce gunshot audio for research. [Maher IEEE DSP 2006](https://www.montana.edu/rmaher/publications/maher_ieeedsp_0906_257-261.pdf), [Procedural synthesis](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-53088-8_4), [Sound of guns](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11042-022-12612-w).

**General noise.** ESC-50 (2,000 clips, 50 classes incl. helicopter, engine, siren, wind, rain, chainsaw, fireworks, thunderstorm; clapping/door/footsteps as hard negatives; official 5 folds), FSD50K (~50k Freesound clips, 200 classes), MUSAN, DEMAND (multichannel real environments). Wind-noise work records real wind on many microphones and mixes at SNR from -20 to +20 dB — wind is capsule turbulence, not a room sound, and is treated as its own distortion type in URGENT. [Low-complexity wind noise reduction](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01821).

## 3. Speech source and the vocal-effort gap

**Lombard effect.** Under 80+ dB SPL people raise F0 and level, tilt energy upward, lengthen vowels. Lombard GRID: 54 speakers × 50 six-word sentences in calm and Lombard conditions (speech-shaped noise at 80 dB SPL through headphones). Audio-visual enhancement systems trained on Lombard speech beat those trained on calm speech on both quality and intelligibility, i.e. the mismatch measurably hurts enhancement, not just ASR. GRID sentences have a tiny vocabulary and fixed syntax, so they are an evaluation asset rather than a training corpus. Recent TTS work synthesises the Lombard effect with continuous control over vocal effort and articulation — evidence that a parametric approximation (level, tilt, duration, F0) captures the first-order effect. [Lombard AV-SE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.12605), [Lombard GRID sentence-type study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.10485), [Lombard TTS 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23176), [Detecting Lombard speech](https://doi.org/10.3390/s23010315).

**Own-voice / hearable capture.** Hearing-protection and hearable research models the *own-voice transfer characteristic* from mouth to each device microphone (outer mic, in-ear mic) and augments a large clean corpus through those measured transfer functions — near-field own voice is high-SNR but band-limited and coloured; far-field mics hear diffuse noise. This is exactly the boom-mic vs ear-cup-reference-mic situation and justifies (a) two output channels and (b) a per-channel transfer function measured from the actual headset. [Speech-dependent augmentation for OVR](https://asmp-eurasipjournals.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s13636-025-00418-1), [OVR training strategies](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.06157).

**Language.** LibriSpeech (1,000 h, 16 kHz, CC BY 4.0) is the default. For an Indian defence context, OpenSLR Hindi (95 h train / 59 speakers), IndicTTS (8 h × 13 languages) and IndicVoices-R (1,704 h, 22 languages) exist and slot in as additional speech piles with speaker IDs. [OpenSLR](https://www.openslr.org/12/), [IndicVoices-R](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.05356).

## 4. Front-end and channel realism

Clipping, band-limiting, codec artefacts and packet loss are first-class distortions in URGENT and are exactly what a tactical radio uplink adds. Microphone mismatch is handled in the literature by per-frequency random magnitude scaling of the STFT or by convolving with device impulse responses; both are cheap. [Device IR augmentation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.07499), [Mic-array generalisation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.12601).

## 5. Learning from real, unlabelled noisy audio

**RemixIT** (2022): a teacher trained on synthetic data estimates speech/noise on *real in-domain* noisy recordings; permuting and remixing the estimates gives bootstrapped pairs for a student — no clean in-domain speech needed. Follow-ups fix the SNR imbalance of the remixes. **Noisy-target training** (Fujimura et al. 2021-2025) trains without any clean speech by adding *more* noise to a noisy target. **Test-time training** (2025) adapts at inference. These are the standard answers to "no combat audio exists": use the pipeline for supervised pre-training, then adapt on real headset recordings. [RemixIT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.08862), [Improved remixing](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13982), [Noisy-target training](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.14854), [CHiME-7 UDASE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03533).

## 6. Generative audio as a data source

Text-to-audio diffusion (AudioLDM family) has been used to augment sound-event-detection sets (Synthio, SynSonic) with filtering by classifiers. DCASE 2024 reports that generated audio "sometimes had large noise and didn't always correspond to the text". For *enhancement* the risk is that the network learns the generator's fingerprint and the test set shares it. Verdict: acceptable for continuous ambience diversity in a pinch, not for transients, and always validated with the crest-factor/attack-time plot. [Synthio](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.02056), [SynSonic](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.18603), [DCASE 2024 T7](https://dcase.community/documents/challenge2024/technical_reports/DCASE2024_Sun_51_t7.pdf).

## 7. Training-schedule techniques that live in the data loader

SNR curriculum (easy-to-hard) and its inverse (ACCAN: lowest SNR first) both report gains in noise robustness; progressive-learning CRNs split enhancement into SNR-improvement stages; SNR-based teacher-student. All are implementable as a schedule on the sampler's SNR distribution. [Curriculum for noise-robust ASR](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06864), [Progressive-learning CRN](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0003682X19309326), [SNR teacher-student](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.14441).

## 8. ANC-path data

Deep ANC work (Zhang & Wang 2020 onward, DNoiseNet, Deep MCANC, speech-preserving ANC 2026) trains on primary-noise recordings paired with measured or simulated secondary paths (loudspeaker to error mic); the pipeline needs the reference-mic channel and, later, a secondary-path IR from the actual ear-cup. Our two-channel output already provides the reference channel. [Deep ANC](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0893608021001258), [Speech-preserving ANC](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.10979).

## 9. What the review changes in our plan

1. ESC-50 has no gunshot class; real gunshots come from the multi-firearm range dataset (uncompressed, timestamped) and UrbanSound8K.
2. Two-channel output plus a measured own-voice transfer function is literature-standard for hearables, not an over-reach.
3. Clipping + codec + packet loss + wind are the URGENT "missing distortions"; keep all of them, config-gated.
4. RemixIT-style self-training on the in-house headset recordings is the accepted bridge to real data and costs one extra script.
5. Generative audio stays out of the impulsive pile.

## 10. Addendum (v2): realistic Lombard and noise-scene composition

**Lombard, quantitatively.** Lombard speech shows raised F0 (typically +30-80 Hz, more at extreme effort), flattened spectral tilt with 5-10 dB more energy in the 1-3 kHz band, raised F1 (wider mouth opening) and an expanded vowel space, longer durations (vowels more than consonants), and a level increase whose slope against ambient level is ~0.3-0.6 dB/dB and depends on the noise type (Lu & Cooke 2008/2009; Garnier et al. 2010; Summers et al. 1988). [Lu & Cooke, F0 and tilt](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167639309001253), [Garnier, acoustic contrasts](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0885230813000557), [Lombard slope / Mandarin SRT](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11938858/). Consequence for synthesis: a whole-spectrum pitch shift is wrong (it moves formants with F0 and sounds like a smaller talker); the parameters must be changed in their own domains. The WORLD vocoder (Morise 2016) separates F0, spectral envelope and aperiodicity for exactly this kind of manipulation and is used for whispered-speech and Lombard voice conversion. [WORLD](https://github.com/mmorise/World), [Lombard VC 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.09310), [Lombard TTS 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23176). Our `lombard_world` applies: F0 x (1.15-1.45), +5-11 dB over 1-3 kHz with a small low cut, F1 warp x (1.04-1.12) below 2.5 kHz, duration x (1.05-1.22), aperiodicity reduced 30-60 % -- all scaled by a strength that is a dose-response of the scene SNR (Lombard slope), so quiet scenes carry calm speech and loud scenes carry strained speech.

**Noise scenes.** Enhancement pipelines for hearing aids simulate one speech source and one to three noise sources in the same room with levels spread over ~10 dB; moving-source simulators (SonicSim, DynamicSound) add trajectories, Doppler and distance attenuation, and report that models trained on static single-noise data degrade on moving sources. [Multi-source scenes](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.01967v1), [SonicSim](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.01481), [DynamicSound](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15433). Our `scene.py` composes 1-3 concurrent layers with category-dependent time envelopes (gust, approach, recede, pass-by, steady), a few percent of Doppler drift, relative levels 2-12 dB below the dominant layer, and per-layer stems for inspection. Full trajectories are not simulated: the model needs to see level and pitch drift, not learn geometry.
